The week's most scenario-relevant evidence came from inside OpenAI's production stack. OpenAI says GPT-5.6 Sol autonomously rewrote and optimized production GPU kernels; those changes, together with broader model-led kernel work, reduced end-to-end serving costs by 20%. It also says Sol designed and ran hundreds of experiments on a draft model, launched and monitored training, intervened after hardware failures and instability, and produced improvements that raised token-generation efficiency by more than 15%. This is concrete, measured AI-assisted AI engineering rather than a benchmark demo, so automation rises from 40 to 42 and compute from 55 to 58. The evidence is still a lab-authored account of bounded inference work—not an independent measurement of algorithmic research across the organization. METR's controlled NanoGPT result therefore remains important counterevidence: returns plateaued below sustained human-equivalent expenditure, and no public evidence yet demonstrates AI 2027's all-inclusive 1.5× R&D multiplier.

Moonshot met its July 27 commitment by releasing Kimi K3's 2.8-trillion-parameter weights and technical report. The release makes a near-frontier, native multimodal model with a one-million-token context inspectable and deployable outside Moonshot. ARC Prize then verified a new open-weight high of 60.4% on the ARC-AGI-2 semi-private set at maximum effort; ARC-AGI-3 testing remains unfinished. This moves capabilities from 66 to 68 and strengthens the scenario's prediction that an open-weight model would match leading systems. It does not independently reproduce Moonshot's more consequential claims about multi-day engineering, chip design, or AI R&D. A retrospective Anthropic disclosure is also a useful boundary condition: three Claude models reached real organizations because an evaluation setup mistakenly exposed the internet, but they used basic techniques and pursued only assigned capture-the-flag goals. The incidents show that operational control is brittle; they do not establish self-directed strategic autonomy.

Race dynamics moved closer to the scenario, though not in its exact form. China's commerce ministry threatened countermeasures against possible US sanctions over alleged model distillation, and Reuters documented military-linked Chinese researchers using outputs from US models to train specialized domestic systems. Separately, Reuters relayed a report that Moonshot has access to about 20,000 Hopper-generation Nvidia chips through Alibaba, while noting that it could not independently confirm the claim and Alibaba denied supplying H200s. Europe announced €10 billion for seven future AI gigafactories, seeking at least €20 billion more in private capital; these are tenders and plans, not deployed clusters. Geopolitics therefore rises from 54 to 57, but there is still no public counterpart to AI 2027's centralized Chinese development zone. Overall alignment rises from 49 to 51: this week offers stronger evidence for bounded recursive efficiency and frontier diffusion, but not for compounding lab-wide takeoff. The authors' latest formal quarterly forecast still places their median fully automated coder in mid-2028, later than the original scenario. The next falsifiable checkpoint is independent Kimi K3 testing on ARC-AGI-3 and long-horizon R&D tasks; a reproducible multi-day result or an audited laboratory productivity multiplier would materially raise the assessment.
